ABOUT FARFRAME

Engineering practical intelligence for physical operations.

Farframe Inc. is an Ontario-based industrial technology company developing software, automation, computer vision, and intelligent systems for operations where physical activity and business information must remain connected.

MISSION

Make complex operations more visible, understandable, and capable.

Farframe develops practical systems that structure operational information, improve control, and create foundations for connected automation and intelligent physical systems.

WHY FARFRAME EXISTS

Connect physical reality with operational information.

Operations-intensive organizations work across two realities: the physical reality of materials, documents, machines, and people, and the digital reality represented inside spreadsheets, databases, and business systems.

When those realities separate, teams lose time, confidence, and operational control. Farframe engineers systems intended to make that connection clearer and more capable.

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

Authority through disciplined engineering.

Farframe’s work is guided by five principles that keep technology grounded in the operation it must serve.

VISION

See the complete system

Understand the operation, its constraints, and its future direction before narrowing the solution.

ENGINEERING

INTELLIGENCE

IMPACT
