DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

DocuExcel

DocuExcel transforms operational and financial documents into structured, usable business data through controlled extraction and review workflows. It is designed for document-heavy processes where accuracy, reviewability, and control over sensitive information matter.

Target workflows

  • Invoices and financial records
  • Packing slips and shipping documents
  • Production and inventory forms
  • Quality and compliance documentation
  • Repetitive spreadsheet-entry workflows

System principles

  • Controlled processing and human-reviewable output
  • Structured Excel, CSV, and business-data delivery
  • Repeatable extraction logic and exception handling
  • Minimal disruption to established workflows

MATERIAL VISIBILITY

Inventory Sentinel

Inventory Sentinel is designed to improve awareness of inventory, material movement, physical location, and discrepancies between real operations and system records. Its direction combines computer vision, operational data, and structured exception workflows.

Problems addressed

  • Materials placed in incorrect locations
  • Movement between areas without reliable records
  • Inventory-count discrepancies
  • Delayed discovery of missing parts
  • Physical inventory that does not match ERP records
  • Limited visibility across receiving, storage, and production

System principles

  • Support existing operational and ERP systems
  • Focus attention on meaningful exceptions
  • Maintain human review and accountability
  • Scale from contained operational areas into broader visibility

SCOPED ENGINEERING

Custom Industrial Systems

Where an operational problem cannot be solved effectively with generic software, Farframe can scope a purpose-built system around the environment, constraints, users, data, and required outcome.

Potential system areas

  • Operational-data and workflow automation
  • Computer-vision inspection and visibility systems
  • Material, asset, and process tracking
  • Integration between existing software and physical operations
  • Exception detection and decision-support systems
  • Connected automation and intelligent infrastructure

How scoping begins

  • Define the repeated operational problem
  • Identify the users, systems, and physical constraints
  • Determine the value and evidence required
  • Design the smallest useful system architecture
  • Expand only after usefulness is demonstrated
